## Findings

### Coca-Cola's 2009 ambition

- Double system revenue to roughly $200 billion by 2020 —
  [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/business/coca-cola-aims-to-double-system-revenue-by-2020-idUSN16268571/)
  (Reuters, Nov 16, 2009). Also: more than double servings to 3B+/day.
- "Coca-Cola aims to triple China sales" — China Beverage News, Nov 2009
  (headline confirmed; site secondary but contemporaneous). The Coca-Cola
  Company 2020 Vision press release (Nov 16–17, 2009) confirms the roadmap.
- Caveat: the original post said "60% of its new growth would come from
  China, India and other emerging markets" — unverifiable as stated. Kent's
  documented claim was that ~60% of the world's *new middle-class
  wealth/consumption growth* would come from emerging nations — a different
  statement. Softened to "most of its new growth" in the post.
- "Reality came in far below the target" — directionally certain (2020 net
  operating revenues were ~$33B; even system revenue never approached
  $200B). Exact 2020 system-revenue figure not nailed down; open question
  if ever needed.
- 2026 epilogue: Coca-Cola "doubles down on localisation in China as sales
  grow" — [FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/08/17/coca-cola-doubles-down-on-localisation-in-china-as-sales-grow/)
  (Aug 17, 2026). Link live and on-topic (Sprite+Tea, prebiotic soda, FIFA
  campaign, local investment).

### Nike: Chamber of Fear, then the long slide

- "Chamber of Fear" (LeBron James vs. kung fu master/dragons) banned by
  Chinese regulators in December 2004 for insulting national dignity —
  confirmed by [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/business/2004/12/07/china-bans-lebron-james-nike-ad/cf7da451-85ca-4885-bacd-058e1b8e0ca1/)
  (Dec 7, 2004), BBC, China Daily, ESPN, Guardian. Nike apologized.
- China business shrunk ~30% since 2021; sales down year-over-year eight
  straight quarters; sportswear market grew 51% in five years; Anta and
  Li-Ning riding the guochao ("China Chic") wave — all confirmed verbatim in
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/nike-china-sales-decline.html)
  (Jul 29, 2026).

### Red Bull localization

- Chinese Red Bull is non-carbonated and positioned close to tea — the
  author's own 2009 reporting from China. Personal observation, no external
  source needed.

### Luckin vs. Starbucks

- Founded 2017; hit 10,000 China stores in June 2023, "surpassing Starbucks
  as the largest coffee chain brand in the country"; 10,829 stores vs.
  Starbucks' 6,480 (Q2 2023) —
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/09/12/how-luckin-coffee-overtook-starbucks-in-china.html)
  (Sep 12, 2023). Post now cites this (replaces marketingtochina.com, an
  agency blog — accurate but off-type for a citation).
- Disagreement note: store-count data shows Luckin first edged past
  Starbucks around 2019–2020, fell behind after the 2020 accounting
  scandal, and re-passed by 2022–2023. The post's earlier "by 2022" was
  defensible; the CNBC-supported "in 2023" milestone is what's cited now.
- Context for scale: Luckin crossed 20,000 stores in July 2024 (Luckin IR
  press release) and reported 24,000+ through 2025; not needed in the post.

### Starbucks–Boyu deal

- 60% stake in China retail operations sold to Boyu Capital; JV announced
  Nov 4, 2025 (Forbes, Starbucks IR) and **closed ~April 2, 2026** —
  [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/starbucks-closes-china-deal-with-boyu-plans-expand-stores-2026-04-02/),
  [Starbucks press release](https://about.starbucks.com/press/2026/starbucks-and-boyu-capitalize-joint-venture-to-accelerate-long-term-growth-in-china/),
  BusinessWire (Mar 30, 2026), SEC 8-K (Apr 2, 2026). The post's AP link
  403s bots but is the canonical AP story of the deal; claim ("in April
  2026, sold 60%") is accurate.

### Tesla vs. BYD shares

- CPCA data via CnEVPost, August 2026 NEV retail: **BYD 23.3%, Tesla 5.0%**
  (Jan–Aug cumulative: BYD 21.7%, Tesla 4.7%) —
  [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/09/09/automakers-aug-2026-share-china-nev-market/)
  (Sep 9, 2026). Post now says "about 5% of China's new-energy-vehicle
  market, against BYD's 23%" with this single source carrying both figures.
- Caveat: earlier draft said "about 6% ... against BYD's 27%" — the linked
  Tesla-only article (7.17% BEV / 4.98% NEV) supported neither number, and
  the 27% BYD figure was stale (2023–24 era). Fixed.
- NEV (not BEV) chosen deliberately: BYD sells both BEVs and PHEVs, so NEV
  is the fair comparison frame; Tesla's BEV-only share (7.17%) would
  flatter Tesla against a PHEV-selling rival.

## Yardstick candidates

- Nike: business shrunk 30% since 2021 while its home market grew 51% —
  shrinking in a rising tide.
- Luckin 10,829 stores vs. Starbucks 6,480 (2023) — the apprentice opening
  ~16 stores a day.
- BYD 23% vs. Tesla 5% NEV share — the student outselling the master nearly
  five to one.
- 1.3 billion people; 11-lane roads (original 2009 texture, kept).

## Disagreements and caveats

- Coca-Cola 60% figure: softened (see above).
- Luckin overtake dating: 2019 vs 2022 vs 2023 depending on metric and
  source (see above).
- India's population passed China's in April 2023 (UN) — post's hedge
  ("passed it in population, but not yet in spending") is safe: China's
  consumer market remains far larger.

## Handoff summary

- Thesis the evidence supports: the 2009 lesson ("understanding beats
  quick wins") held — the bar moved from "don't offend" to "out-localize
  the locals", and the brands that lost (Nike, Starbucks, Tesla) lost to
  immersion, not to marketing.
- Best facts (3–5, with links): Nike −30% since 2021 vs. +51% market (CNBC,
  Jul 2026); Chamber of Fear ban (Washington Post, Dec 2004); Luckin's
  10,829-vs-6,480 overtake (CNBC, Sep 2023); Starbucks–Boyu 60% close
  (Reuters, Apr 2026); BYD 23.3% vs. Tesla 5.0% NEV (CnEVPost, Sep 2026).
