## Findings

### Cargo cults and Feynman

- Feynman's 1974 Caltech commencement address "Cargo Cult Science" —
  [calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm](https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm)
  (Caltech Engineering & Science, 1974). Link live and on-topic; "worth
  reading once a year" stands.

### Twitter, gamification, Foursquare

- Twitter's 140-character limit came from SMS constraints (160 chars minus
  username) — well-established history, unlinked in post (fine, common
  knowledge at this point).
- Foursquare shut down its City Guides app in December 2024 (announced
  October 2024). The Swarm check-in app lives on. Post says "shut its app
  down in 2024" — accurate enough; the precision "city-guide app" is
  available if ever wanted.

### MIT "GenAI Divide" (95%)

- ~5% of enterprise GenAI pilots achieve rapid revenue acceleration; 95%
  fall short — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/)
  (Fortune, Aug 18, 2025), interview with report lead author Aditya
  Challapally (MIT NANDA, *The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025*).
- Caveat: the "$30–40 billion of investment, zero return" wording is from the
  MIT NANDA report itself; the Fortune piece covers the 95% finding but not
  the dollar figure. Post's attribution ("MIT researchers reported") is
  accurate; the link supports the 95%, not the $ number.

### Gartner agent washing

- Gartner press release, June 25, 2025 —
  [gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-...](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027).
  Gartner 403s bots, so the release text was confirmed via independent
  coverage quoting it verbatim: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/over-40-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-scrapped-by-2027-gartner-says-2025-06-25/)
  (Jun 25, 2025), Economic Times, Hindu BusinessLine, SDxCentral.
- Confirmed: "agent washing" is Gartner's coinage (rebranding AI assistants,
  RPA, chatbots without substantial agentic capabilities); "only about 130 of
  the thousands of agentic AI vendors are real"; "over 40% of agentic AI
  projects will be canceled by end of 2027".

### SEC v. Saniger (Nate)

- SEC Litigation Release 26282 — [sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/litigation-releases/lr-26282)
  (SEC, Apr 11, 2025; complaint filed Apr 9, 2025, S.D.N.Y.). Confirmed:
  charged founder/former CEO Albert Saniger; raised "over $42 million";
  marketed Nate as AI-driven while "contract employees" manually processed
  orders.
- Caveat: the litigation release does not name countries. Philippines appears
  in the [SEC complaint PDF](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2025/comp26282.pdf)
  and DOJ SDNY press release (Apr 2025, bot-walled to fetchers);
  Philippines + Romania together in [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/107510-founder-nate-app-faces-fraud-charge-using-ai.html)
  and PC Gamer coverage. Post now links TechSpot for the geographic detail.

### Klarna

- AI assistant did the work of 700 customer-support agents; company later
  reassigned workers back into support roles —
  [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/klarna-reassigns-workers-to-customer-support-after-ai-quality-concerns-2025-9)
  (BI, Sep 2025). Caveat: BI's correction notes the CEO's "went too far"
  remark was about cost-cutting broadly; the quality framing rests on his
  Bloomberg interview quote ("what you end up having is lower quality").
  Post's telling ("reversed course when quality dropped") remains accurate.
